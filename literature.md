# Literature

The works cited in the papers and notes of this repository, with one sentence on what each contains that is
relevant here. Copies of the papers are not included (copyright).

## Point-vortex rings on surfaces of constant curvature

- **J. Montaldi, T. Tokieda**, "Deformation of geometry and bifurcations of vortex rings", in *Recent Trends in
  Dynamical Systems*, Springer Proc. Math. Stat. 35 (2013) 335–370, doi:10.1007/978-3-0348-0451-6_14;
  arXiv:1210.5662. A one-parameter family of point-vortex models on the sphere, the plane and the hyperbolic plane,
  the stability thresholds of the regular ring in that family (the sinh model at λ < 0), the bifurcations at the
  threshold, and Conjecture 13 on stability at the bifurcation point.
- **S. Boatto**, "Curvature perturbations and stability of a ring of vortices", *Discrete Contin. Dyn. Syst. Ser. B*
  10 (2008) 349–375, doi:10.3934/dcdsb.2008.10.349. Ring stability as a function of curvature, including the
  hyperbolic surface with the image ("tanh") Green's function; the Hessian in circulant block form and numerical
  stability ranges. See also `tanh/boatto_check/`.
- **J. Montaldi, C. Nava-Gaxiola**, "Point vortices on the hyperbolic plane", *J. Math. Phys.* 55 (2014) 102702;
  arXiv:1403.2138. Point vortices on the hyperbolic plane with the Hamiltonian in the form used for the tanh model;
  relative equilibria and stability for two and three vortices.
- **C. Nava-Gaxiola**, *Point vortices on the hyperboloid*, PhD thesis, University of Manchester (2013),
  eprints.maths.manchester.ac.uk/2022. Point-vortex dynamics on the hyperboloid model of the hyperbolic plane.
- **Y. Kimura**, "Vortex motion on surfaces with constant curvature", *Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A* 455 (1999) 245–259,
  doi:10.1098/rspa.1999.0311. A unified Hamiltonian formulation of point-vortex motion on the sphere and the
  hyperbolic plane.
- **S. Hwang, S.-C. Kim**, "Relative equilibria of point vortices on the hyperbolic sphere", *J. Math. Phys.* 54
  (2013) 063101, doi:10.1063/1.4811454. Relative equilibria of point vortices on the hyperbolic plane, including
  rings of vortices.
- **S. Boatto, J. Koiller**, "Vortices on closed surfaces", arXiv:0802.4313. Point-vortex dynamics on closed
  surfaces through the Green's and Robin functions.

## The planar ring and its nonlinear stability

- **T. H. Havelock**, "The stability of motion of rectilinear vortices in ring formation", *Phil. Mag.* (7) 11
  (1931). Linear stability of the planar ring: stable for N ≤ 7.
- **H. Lamb**, *Hydrodynamics*, 6th ed., Cambridge University Press (1932), §156. The classical account of the
  vortex ring in the plane.
- **L. G. Kurakin, V. I. Yudovich**, "The stability of stationary rotation of a regular vortex polygon", *Chaos* 12
  (2002) 574–595, doi:10.1063/1.1482175. Nonlinear stability of the planar regular vortex polygon, including N = 7.
- **D. Schmidt**, "The stability of the Thomson heptagon", *Regul. Chaotic Dyn.* 9 (2004) 519–528,
  doi:10.1070/RD2004v009n04ABEH000294. Nonlinear stability of the planar heptagon.
- **L. G. Kurakin**, "On the nonlinear stability of regular vortex polygons and polyhedrons on a sphere", *Doklady
  Physics* 48 (2003) 84–89, doi:10.1134/1.1560737; and "On nonlinear stability of the regular vortex systems on a
  sphere", *Chaos* 14 (2004) 592–602, doi:10.1063/1.1764432. Nonlinear stability of regular vortex configurations
  on the sphere.
- **A. V. Borisov, A. A. Kilin**, "Stability of Thomson's configurations of vortices on a sphere", *Regul. Chaotic
  Dyn.* 5 (2000); arXiv:nlin/0503068. Stability of rings of vortices on the sphere.

## Stability of relative equilibria

- **J. Montaldi**, "Persistence and stability of relative equilibria", *Nonlinearity* 10 (1997) 449–466,
  doi:10.1088/0951-7715/10/2/009. Persistence and stability of relative equilibria of Hamiltonian systems with
  symmetry.
- **G. W. Patrick, M. Roberts, C. Wulff**, "Stability of Poisson equilibria and Hamiltonian relative equilibria by
  energy methods", *Arch. Ration. Mech. Anal.* 174 (2004) 301–344, doi:10.1007/s00205-004-0322-9;
  arXiv:math/0201239. Energy-momentum stability criteria for Hamiltonian relative equilibria.
- **J. Montaldi, M. Rodríguez-Olmos**, "Hamiltonian relative equilibria with continuous isotropy",
  arXiv:1509.04961 (2015). Stability of relative equilibria for noncompact symmetry groups.
