# Havelock directions map (agent B, 2026-10-10)

## Directions
1. **Flat torus.** Small rings are not relative equilibria. The residual scales with the point stabiliser: C2 ∝ ξ, C4 ∝ ξ², C6 ∝ ξ³ (probe confirmed). The right object is a relative periodic orbit. Already done for the torus of revolution: Sakajo–Shimizu. Interest: low–medium.
2. **Compact hyperbolic surfaces.** No ring-stability work found at genus ≥ 2. The Bolza C8 Weierstrass point is good; C3 and C2 points give O(ξ^{3/2}) and O(ξ) defects (prediction only). Full model: Grotta-Ragazzo–Gustafsson–Koiller, RCD 2024. Needs bolzakit. Interest: medium (math).
3. **Rotating BEC shells: the real physical audience.** Literature: CAL Nature 2022; Padavić 2005.13030; review 2512.13858; Massignan–Fetter. Open question: N-ring plus anti-ring thresholds vs latitude and rotation Ω on a shell or ellipsoid. Interest: high.
4. **He films and liquid crystals on catenoids or saddles.** Does negative curvature push N_crit above 7? Medium.
5. **Multi-ring configurations on S².** Largely closed (LMR; Constantineau–García-Azpeitia–García-Naranjo–Lessard 2309.04320 has interval-certified branches). The H² analogues are incremental.
6. **Generalised kernels.** Open pieces: K0-with-centre, Δ*(5) = 2.37764, Δ*(6) = 0.48360. Medium.
7. **Nonlinear stability at threshold.** Montaldi–Tokieda Conjecture 13 (the n-gon is stable at the bifurcation point) has no resolution found. Kurakin–Yudovich-type quartic coefficient at ξ*(8) = 8 − 3√7. Interest: high (math).
8. **Physical H² vortex realisation.** None exists.
9. **Certified handbook.** Precedent: 2309.04320 (S² only).
