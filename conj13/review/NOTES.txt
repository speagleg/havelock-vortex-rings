Odd n (odd_general.py, odd_general_run.py, odd_assemble.py; outputs odd_general.out, odd_assemble.out):
    symbolic in n via canonical coords (theta, w = u/(1+lam u)), J3 linear; ansatz delta = A cos(l phi) +
    A^2 p cos(phi), psi = A^2 s sin(phi); offset sums over 2n-th roots of unity evaluated by residues at
    t = +-1 (kernel n t^{n-1}/(t^n - s) expanded with binomial(n,k) as polynomials in n), plus n*(t^0 coeff
    at infinity); Laurent degree at 0 and infinity <= 0 for every piece, so valid for every n; Fourier
    selection valid for n >= 5. Result: kernel eigenvalue vanishes exactly on the threshold, A^3 term = 0,
    free mode-1 curvature a2 = n(n-1)(1-lam)^2(1+lam)^2/8 > 0, and
    c4 = (n-1)(n-3)(n^2-9n+38)/(128n) identically modulo (n-7)(1+lam^2) + 2(n+1)lam = 0 (n != 7),
    and exactly 9/14 at n = 7. Numerically matches the independent Cartesian KKT at n = 5..17.
    Remaining inputs: (i) strict positivity of the reduced Hessian on all non-kernel modes at b_n for
    every odd n (MT's spectral analysis, not re-proved here); (ii) the stability theorem (Prop. 9).

Prop. 9: MT cite [17] = Montaldi, Nonlinearity 10 (1997) 449-466, whose setting is a compact group G
    (abstract), as for the sphere (n = 4, 5, 6). For SE(2) (n = 7) and SL(2,R) (n >= 8) we give a
    self-contained argument. Montaldi-Rodriguez-Olmos arXiv:1509.04961 Thm 3.6 covers noncompact G with a
    G_mu-invariant inner product under formal stability (definite Hessian on N). Their proof uses the Morse
    lemma for Lyapunov stability of 0 in the family h(rho, .) on N; for a degenerate (quartic) minimum a
    strict-minimum + continuity-in-rho argument takes its place (written out in gaps/PROOF.txt).
