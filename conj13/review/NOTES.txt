Prop. 9: MT cite [17] = Montaldi, Nonlinearity 10 (1997) 449-466, which assumes G COMPACT (abstract).
    Fine for the sphere (n = 4, 5, 6); NOT applicable as stated to SE(2) (n = 7) or SL(2,R) (n >= 8).
    Montaldi-Rodriguez-Olmos arXiv:1509.04961 Thm 3.6 covers noncompact G with a G_mu-invariant inner
    product, but only under formal stability (definite Hessian on N); the degenerate (quartic) case
    is not covered verbatim. Their proof uses the Morse lemma only for Lyapunov stability of 0 in the
    family h(rho, .) on N; a strict-minimum + continuity-in-rho argument should replace it, but this
    has to be written and checked. Also MT's claim in the proof of Prop. 9 that Lemma 8's hypotheses
    on M_mu and on N are equivalent is false beyond second order (this is P1).
